## What to keep doing

The project's own record of this is `docs/PRACTICES.md`. These are the points an outside reader
would single out, not a restatement of it.

- **Every number carries its interval, and a withdrawal is published.** Kraków and Melbourne were
  reported as small positives and are now reported, in the README's own summary, as not
  distinguishable from zero.
- **A world with known causes.** The synthetic sweep shows each detector answering its own cause
  and ignoring the other: PSI moves 1.77× under covariate drift and 1.00× under concept drift;
  performance drift moves 1.01× and 5.92×. Six real cities cannot show that, and the page says so.
- **The headline was cut by the project's own ablation**, from +49.4% to +9.7% [+8.0, +26.0], with
  the decomposition published: 21.6 points from the shipped `alpha`, 18.0 from linearity.
- **Five failures, one reason.** Every mechanism that adjusts when the loop acts, or how hard it
  judges, leaves the number of attempts unpriced, so raising a bar buys a luckier winner rather
  than fewer bad promotions. That diagnosis is the most transferable thing in the repository.
- **Check for boundary optima before believing a tuned comparison** (`HANDOFF.md`, 2026-08-14). A
  grid that put the tree's optimum at its most-regularised corner in every dimension was
  truncating the answer, not containing it.

### 3. Price the number of attempts

The diagnosis behind finding 4 names what none of the five mechanisms touched: the loop retries
until a challenger passes, and nothing accounts for how many challengers were tried. The mechanism
it implies, and nobody has built, is a promotion margin that grows with the number of challengers
tried since the last promotion. That corrects the winner's curse at its source, where every
mechanism so far raised one bar for every attempt alike.

This is a suggestion from outside the project, not one of its findings. Run it the way the sweeps
were run: six cities, an interval on every arm, and the predicted answer written down before the
replay (`docs/PRACTICES.md`, "Carried into the next project").
